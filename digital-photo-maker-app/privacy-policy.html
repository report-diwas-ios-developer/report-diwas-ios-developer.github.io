Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 17, 2026

Privacy First: Digital Photo Maker app is designed to work entirely offline. We do not collect, store, or transmit your biometric data or photos to any external servers.

1. Data Processing

All photo processing, background removal, and biometric alignment are performed locally on your device. We do not have access to your camera roll or the photos you process within the app.

2. No Data Collection

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. Because the app works offline, no data is transmitted to us or any third parties.

3. Image Storage

The photos you create are only saved to your device's local gallery when you explicitly click the "Save" button. You have full control over your files.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app does not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs that collect your data.
